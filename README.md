# SMRJ Youth Council 2027 — The Ascent of Ideas
### Official Website Architecture & Master Design System

The digital presence for the maiden edition of **SMRJ Youth Council 2027**, hosted by **Seth M.R. Jaipuria School, Gomti Nagar, Lucknow** on **16—17 April 2027**.

Designed to meet and exceed Site of the Day standards on Awwwards and CSS Design Awards, rooted in the core concept:
> **"An idea is not an endpoint. It is the beginning of a journey."**  
> *वसुधैव कुटुम्बकम् — The World is One Family.*

---

## 🏛️ Creative & Technical Highlights

1. **Signature 3D Monumental Spiral Staircase (Three.js)**
   - Parametric architectural helical staircase with custom stone fluting, bronze balustrades, and soft purple ambient shadows.
   - Three illuminated landings representing the three phases of the summit (*Landing 01: Deliberation*, *Landing 02: Collaboration*, *Landing 03: Ratified Youth Declaration*).
   - Scroll-linked and pointer-reactive camera paths with real-time landing inspection.

2. **The Connected World 3D Interactive Globe (Three.js)**
   - Represents the Sanskrit ethos *Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam*.
   - Interactive sphere with celestial gold orbital rings, great-circle connecting arcs, and real-time delegatory hub inspection.

3. **Eight Master-Crafted Pages**
   - `01 Home`: Hero 3D Ascent, Convocation Editorial Split, Interactive Horizontal Journey, Living Deep-Purple Countdown, Participation Ethos, School Legacy Preview, Custom Identity Footer.
   - `02 Our School`: Legacy of Learning, Sri Aurobindo quote, Animated Editorial Statistics, World's Top 10 Recognition, 3D Connected Globe, Interactive Leadership Message Switcher, Campus Lightbox Gallery.
   - `03 Youth Council`: Ideas Into Impact, Institutional Genesis, Dual-Stage Architecture (Phase I & II interactive switcher), Eligibility, and Official Prospectus CTA.
   - `04 Resources`: Resource Archive (6 reference volumes), Locked Core Challenge Embargo Countdown, *"Vote for What Matters"* Team Statement Archive with verification safeguards, and Interactive Campus Schematics with wayfinding pins.
   - `05 Sponsors`: Editorial Tiered Sponsor Wall (Title, Powered By, Associate, Knowledge, Supporting, Media) with authentic brand colors and external portal links.
   - `06 Our Team`: Faculty Advisor Mrs. Bhavya Jain, Directors Tavisha Gattani & Rudraa Singh with dual portraits and joint convocation, and an 8-department interactive Secretariat Index.
   - `07 Contact`: 5 verified contact channels (Email, Phone, Maps) and a validated anti-spam transmission form.
   - `08 Register`: 3-stage delegation accreditation (School info, 3-member delegate validation, review & certification, registration reference ID generation, summary print, and celebration confetti).

4. **Visual Identity & Color Palette**
   - Primary Purple: `#563F6A`
   - Deep Purple: `#3B284B` / `#2A1B36`
   - Radiant Gold: `#EDC868`
   - Cream / Paper Canvas: `#FAF8F5`
   - Ink: `#2A2330`
